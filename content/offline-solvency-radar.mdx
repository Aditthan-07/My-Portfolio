Modern wealth management applications routinely require uploading sensitive banking credentials, balance sheets, and tax histories to third-party cloud servers. For many retail investors in emerging markets, this data exposure is a significant barrier.

When building **FinSight AI (Investor App)**, the goal was to provide institutional-grade solvency analysis and portfolio optimization directly on user hardware.

## The 5-Axis Solvency Radar

The core solvency engine evaluates financial health across five orthogonal dimensions:

1. **Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio:** Evaluates fixed obligations against verified monthly inflows.
2. **Liquidity Coverage:** Measures liquid emergency reserves against recurring survival expenses.
3. **Credit Health:** Evaluates borrowing utilization and repayment consistency.
4. **Savings Rate:** Assesses discretionary surplus retention over a rolling 12-month window.
5. **Volatility Buffer:** Stress-tests asset durability against historical market drawdowns.

The engine computes an aggregated Solvency Score between 0 and 100 without transmitting raw figures off-device.

## ML Risk-Adaptive Equities Basket

Rather than applying static 60/40 asset allocations, FinSight AI utilizes a trained **Scikit-Learn Random Forest** classifier. The model takes personal risk tolerance, financial runway, and market benchmark volatility (NIFTY 50) as inputs to construct dynamic equity baskets:

- **Defensive Capital Preservation:** Large-cap equities with low beta and high dividend yields.
- **Balanced Growth:** Hybrid allocations balancing index funds with sector leaders.
- **Aggressive Accumulation:** Strategic exposure to high-growth emerging sectors.

## Mobile Architecture & Standalone APK

The mobile client is engineered with **Expo SDK 51** and **React Native 0.74**, utilizing TypeScript for end-to-end type safety. Key architectural decisions include:

- **Local State Sovereignty:** Financial parameters are cached securely in device storage.
- **RESTful FastAPI Microservice:** Quantitative endpoints provide caching layers for live market data (yfinance) and validation contracts via Pydantic schemas.
- **Standalone APK Release:** Built through EAS with zero external dependency requirements for core auditing.
